# Code of Conduct

Pushify follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, or publishing others' private information.
- Keep discussions on topic and help newcomers find their way.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and to anyone
representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Email **support@pushify.dev** with what happened, where, and links if you have them. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or block people who break
this code, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
